# University Computer Club 💻

The University Computer Club is a community for students who are passionate about technology, programming, and innovation. Our goal is to help students improve their technical skills and collaborate on projects.

## Activities
- Programming workshops
- Hackathons and coding competitions
- Tech talks and seminars
- Collaborative projects

## Mission
To build a strong tech community where students can learn, share knowledge, and grow together in the field of computer science and technology.

University Computer Club Members
